## Make yourself at home

Click any text to edit it. **Ctrl+B** makes text bold. **Ctrl+I** makes it italic.

- Open a document with **Ctrl+O**
- Save your work with **Ctrl+S**
- Switch to Markdown source with **Ctrl+/**

> Everything stays in ordinary Markdown files, on your computer.
